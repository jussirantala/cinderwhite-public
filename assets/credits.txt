Third-party audio used in snowshooter (assets/audio/*.mp3)
===========================================================
All sources are CC0 1.0 or public domain. Every file was converted to mono
80 kbps MP3 with ffmpeg, trimmed, peak-normalised and (for *-loop files)
cross-faded into a seamless loop; some were filtered (high/low-pass).

Size optimizations (2026-10-23, content unchanged, still CC0): Poly Haven normal maps
(*-nor_gl.jpg) and glove-leather-rough.jpg downscaled 1k -> 512; other 1k jpgs re-encoded at
quality ~80; satara-night-1k.hdr downscaled to 512x256 (used only for PMREM env lighting);
adventurer.glb deduped/pruned/resampled/quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization); flamethrower.glb
normal+ORM textures downscaled to 512 and quantized.
